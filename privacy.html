Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Riposte is an iPhone app for competitive fencers, their parents and their coaches. It follows tournaments live, keeps a private bout journal, connects students with their coach and shows ratings and rankings. This policy explains what information Riposte handles, why, and the choices you have. "Riposte", "we" and "us" mean the people who make the app. You can reach us at l1y7iam@gmail.com.

Many fencers are children and teenagers. Section 5 explains how we handle children's information and what parents can do. Parents should also read our Notice to parents.

The short version
  • Before you make an account, Riposte asks your birth year. We keep only an age group, never your birth date.
  • Children under 13 can't make an account. A parent sets Riposte up for them. Without that, they can only look around as a guest.
  • Your journals are private. A fencer you write about can never see what you wrote about them.
  • Coaches can only message students who accepted their link, or members of their club. Every chat has block and report.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

Your age

Before any account is made, Riposte asks for your birth year. On iOS 26 and later it can also use Apple's Declared Age Range, which tells us an age range without a birth date. From this we store only an age group on your account: under 13, 13 to 15, 16 to 17, or adult. We use it to decide what you can do in the app. We never store your birth date.

Your account

  • Guest. You can look around without signing up. We create an anonymous account with a random ID. It holds no name, email or birth date. A guest can follow one fencer to get event alerts, and can't change anything else.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google. When you sign up, we receive the account identifier and email address from Apple or Google, plus your name if you share it. With Apple you can hide your email, and we then receive a private relay address. We never see your Apple or Google password.
  • Role and preferences. Whether you use Riposte as a fencer, parent or coach, the fencers you follow, and your notification settings.

Parent accounts

A parent account is an adult account set up to manage a child's use of Riposte. When a parent agrees to our Notice to parents, we record which parent agreed, for which fencer, when, and which version of the notice they read.

Claiming a fencer profile

To link an account to a competition record, you enter the fencing federation member number, last name and birth year. A parent does this for their child. We use these only to match the right fencer and to check the claim. The member number is never shown to other users. The birth year is checked against the public record and not stored on your account.

Your bout journal

For each bout you choose to journal, Riposte stores:

  • the opponent and event;
  • the score and result;
  • tags and ratings you pick, such as handedness, speed, aggressiveness and tactical "chips";
  • your written notes and the time you wrote them.

Journals are saved on your phone and synced to our server, so they survive a reinstall or a new phone.

Coaches, lessons and messages

  • Coach links. Which coaches you are linked to, and whether you accepted the link.
  • Lessons. Lesson requests, bookings and their notes, and a coach's schedule and open hours.
  • Messages. Messages between a coach and their students, and a club's announcements to its members.
  • Blocks and reports. Who you blocked, and any report you send us about a chat.

The coach assistant

Coaches can ask an AI assistant to help with their schedule. We store the coach's chat with the assistant and short notes it keeps about the coach's habits, until the coach clears them.

The parent assistant

Parents can ask an AI assistant which competitions suit their children. It answers only from the competitions and qualification rules Riposte has already picked for each child. We don't store the parent's chat; we only count how many questions were asked each day, to keep usage fair.

Camera and microphone

  • Camera. Used only when you tap Scan, to read a coach's or club's QR code. We don't keep any photos or video.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. Used only for the coach assistant's voice input, while you hold the mic button. Your speech is turned into text on your phone when your phone supports it. Otherwise Apple's speech recognition service does it. We don't record or keep audio. Only the text is sent to us.

Device and notification data

  • Push tokens. If you allow notifications, Apple gives the app a push token so we can send you alerts, for example pools posted or your next DE bout. This also covers Live Activity updates on your Lock Screen.
  • Delivery records. To measure how fast alerts arrive, we keep brief delivery records (time sent and Apple's delivery status).
  • App version. We record your app version so we can tell you when an update is available.

Public competition information

Riposte shows tournament information that organizers and results sites already publish openly. It comes from askFRED, USA Fencing rankings, FencingTracker, Fencing Time Live and results files uploaded by organizers:

  • names, clubs and birth years as published;
  • entry lists, pool assignments and scores;
  • DE tables and final placings;
  • ratings and national rankings.

A fencer profile exists for everyone in those results, whether or not they use Riposte. From these results Riposte calculates a performance score and rank for each fencer. If you would like a correction or removal, contact us (see section 8).

What we don't collect

Riposte does not access your contacts, photos, location or health data. It doesn't use the advertising identifier. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. How we use information

  • To run the app: follow fencers and events, show live pools, bout order, DE tables and results, and send the alerts you asked for.
  • To apply the age rules in section 5.
  • To save and sync your journals and calculate your scouting progress.
  • To link an account to a fencer profile once it is claimed.
  • To let coaches and their students book lessons and message each other.
  • To calculate ratings, rankings and win-chance estimates from public results.
  • To keep the service secure, review reports, prevent abuse and fix problems.

We don't use anyone's information for advertising or to build marketing profiles.

4. Third parties

We use a small number of providers to run Riposte. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, sign-in and server functions. All the information in section 1 is stored there.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications and Live Activities, speech recognition when your phone can't do it itself, and app distribution through TestFlight and the App Store. Apple receives your push token and the alert text it delivers.
  • Google: Sign in with Google, only if you choose it.
  • Anthropic: two features.
    • AI scouting. When you ask for an AI summary of an opponent, the journals you wrote about that opponent are sent: dates, event, result, score, aggressiveness rating and your notes, plus the opponent's name. If you never ask, nothing is sent.
    • Coach assistant. Anthropic's model may answer the coach's assistant requests (see NVIDIA below for what is sent).
    • Parent assistant. When a parent asks a question, it receives the question and, for each child it's about: their first name (for a child under 13, a stand-in such as "Child 1" instead, and the name is also removed from the question), age group, weapon, region, season goals and the public competitions picked for them. Never a last name, birth year, member number or contact details.
    Under Anthropic's commercial terms this data is not used to train their models.
  • NVIDIA (NIM): the AI model the coach and parent assistants use when Anthropic's isn't available. For the parent assistant it receives exactly what Anthropic would (above). For the coach assistant: When a coach writes or speaks to the assistant, it receives the coach's message and name, their recent chat with the assistant and its notes, their lessons, open hours and busy times, and their students' first names and Riposte IDs. Students' last names are sent only for students 13 and over. No journals, messages or contact details are sent.

Nothing that identifies a fencer under 13 is sent to an AI service: no name, birth year or member number. The parent assistant refers to them only as "Child 1", "Child 2" and so on, with their age group, weapon and region, and puts the name back on your phone. AI scouting is never used for opponents under 13, and is off for users under 18 unless they turn it on.

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or the service.

5. Children and parents

Fencing has youth events, and many Riposte users are under 18. This section explains how we follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws.

Age rules in the app

  • Under 13. A child can't make an account. They can look around as a guest, which stores no name, email or birth date, and follow one fencer for event alerts. A parent must set Riposte up for them through a parent account.
  • 13 to 17. Teens can sign up themselves, with a parent's permission (see our Terms). A coach can only message them after the teen, or their parent, accepts the coach's link. AI scouting is off unless they turn it on. Coach accounts need age 16 or older.
  • Guests can look but not change anything: no journals, messages, coach links, bookings, check-in or friends.

What we collect from or about children

  • Public results. Names, clubs, birth years, ratings and results already published by organizers and results sites, for every fencer including children.
  • Through a parent account. The member number, last name and birth year the parent enters to claim their child's profile, and the information the child's use of Riposte creates: journals, coach links, lesson requests and bookings, and messages with their coach.
  • From a child guest. Only a random account ID, the one fencer they follow and a push token if notifications are on.

We use this information only to run Riposte for the child and their parent. We don't use it for advertising, we don't sell it, and we never send it to an AI service.

How parents consent

An adult (18 or older) signs up with Apple or Google and chooses to set up Riposte for their child. They read our Notice to parents and agree to it. Then they claim their child's fencer profile with the member number, last name and birth year. We record which parent agreed, for which fencer, when, and the notice version.

We don't ask for more information than a feature needs. A child doesn't have to give more information to take part.

Parents' rights

  • Review. See your child's journals, coach links, bookings and messages in the app, or email us for a copy.
  • Withdraw consent. In Profile → Settings, withdraw your consent. This deletes the child-related data your account holds (journals, coach links, lesson requests and bookings, and coach message threads) and releases the claim on your child's profile.
  • Delete. Delete the whole account in Profile → Settings → Delete account, or email us and we'll do it.
  • Refuse further collection. Withdrawing consent stops any further collection for that child.
  • Correct or hide a public profile. Email us and we'll correct or hide your child's public profile in Riposte.

We reply to parents' requests within 30 days. We may ask you to show you are the parent before we share or change a child's information.

If we learn we have a child's information without consent

If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information without a parent's consent, email us. We will delete it.

6. How long we keep information

  • Account, journals, coach links, lessons, messages and preferences: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Parent consent records: while the consent stands, and deleted with the account.
  • Coach assistant chat and notes: until the coach clears them or deletes the account.
  • Notifications in the app: 30 days after they are read or delivered, and 60 days at most.
  • Push delivery records: 14 days.
  • "Call coach" requests: 7 days.
  • Pool and strip reports you type in: 30 days.
  • Guest accounts: a guest with no push token that hasn't opened Riposte for 7 days stops following its fencer and stops getting alerts.
  • Server logs of data imports: 14 days.
  • Backups held by our hosting provider expire on their normal schedule, usually within a few weeks.
  • Public competition results: kept, because they are public records. When an account is deleted, its claim on a fencer profile and its badges are released.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and its data: journals, follows, coach links, lessons, messages, tokens and preferences.
  • Edit or delete a journal at any time in the app.
  • Block or report anyone you chat with, from the chat.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • Camera, microphone and speech recognition: turn them off in iOS Settings. The rest of the app keeps working.
  • Access, correction or a copy of your data: email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, for example in California or the European Economic Area, you may have further rights. These include asking us to access, correct, delete or move your data, objecting to some processing, and complaining to your local data protection authority. We honour these requests wherever you live. Where the GDPR applies, we process your data to provide the service you asked for (contract), for our legitimate interest in running and securing the app, and with your consent (or your parent's) for optional features such as notifications and AI scouting.

8. Public results about you

Competition results are published by tournament organizers. If you are a fencer, or the parent of one, and want your public results corrected or hidden in Riposte, email us from the address linked to your account, or include enough detail for us to identify the record. We'll reply within 30 days.

9. Security

We keep a written information security program. In short:

  • Access controls. Database rules limit each person to their own private data. Only the developer can reach the production systems.
  • Encryption. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our hosting provider, Supabase.
  • Least privilege. Server keys with full access stay on the server and are used only by the functions that need them. The app only holds a public key that the database rules restrict.
  • Less data. We keep only an age group, not birth dates, and delete short-lived records on the schedule in section 6.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you as required by law if a breach affects you.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes we'll also tell you in the app before they take effect. If a change affects children's information, we'll ask parents to agree again.
