Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Riposte is an iPhone app for competitive fencers. It follows tournaments live, keeps a private bout journal and shows ratings and rankings. This policy explains what information Riposte handles, why, and the choices you have. "Riposte", "we" and "us" mean the people who make the app. You can reach us at l1y7iam@gmail.com.

The short version
  • You can use Riposte as a guest. Signing in with Apple or Google is optional.
  • Your journals are private. Nobody can read your notes. A fencer you write about can never see what you wrote about them.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

Your account

  • Guest account. When you first open Riposte we create an anonymous account with a random ID, so your follows and journals can be saved. It contains no name or email.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google (optional). When you choose to sign in, we receive the account identifier and email address from Apple or Google, plus your name if you share it. With Apple you can hide your email, and we then receive a private relay address. We never see your Apple or Google password.
  • Role and preferences. Whether you use Riposte as a fencer, parent or coach, the fencer you follow, and your notification settings.

Claiming your fencer profile (optional)

To link your account to your own competition record, you enter your fencing federation member number, last name and birth year. We use these only to match you to the right fencer and to check the claim. Your member number is never shown to other users.

Your bout journal

For each bout you choose to journal, Riposte stores:

  • the opponent and event;
  • the score and result;
  • tags and ratings you pick, such as handedness, speed, aggressiveness and tactical "chips";
  • your written notes and the time you wrote them.

Journals are saved on your phone and synced to our server, so they survive a reinstall or a new phone.

Device and notification data

  • Push tokens. If you allow notifications, Apple gives the app a push token so we can send you alerts, for example pools posted or your next DE bout. This also covers Live Activity updates on your Lock Screen.
  • Delivery records. To measure how fast alerts arrive, we keep brief delivery records (time sent and Apple's delivery status). We delete them after 14 days.
  • App version. We record your app version so we can tell you when an update is available.

Public competition information

Riposte shows tournament information that organizers and results sites already publish openly:

  • entry lists, pool assignments and scores;
  • DE tables, final placings and clubs;
  • ratings and national rankings.

From these results Riposte calculates a performance score and rank for each fencer. This information is about fencers in general, not only Riposte users. If you would like a correction or removal, contact us (see section 7).

What we don't collect

Riposte does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone or health data. It contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. How we use information

  • To run the app: follow fencers and events, show live pools, bout order, DE tables and results, and send the alerts you asked for.
  • To save and sync your journals and calculate your scouting progress.
  • To link your account to your fencer profile once you claim it.
  • To calculate ratings, rankings and win-chance estimates from public results.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.

3. Who can see what

InformationWho can see it
Your journal notesOnly you. If you turn on "share with coach" for a bout, your linked coach can read that bout's journal.
Journals other people write about youNever you, and never any other user. They are hidden from the fencer they describe.
Scouting tags (e.g. "left-handed", "fast attacks")Combined anonymously with other fencers' tags and shown to other users scouting that opponent, without saying who added them. Never shown to the fencer they describe. Your written notes are never shared.

Public results, ratings and rankingsEveryone, as on the organizers' own sites.

4. Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run Riposte. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, sign-in and server functions.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications and Live Activities, and app distribution through TestFlight and the App Store.
  • Google: Sign in with Google, only if you choose it.
  • Anthropic: the optional AI scouting summary. When you ask for an AI summary of an opponent, the journals you wrote about that opponent are sent to Anthropic's API to write the summary: scores, ratings, tags and notes. Under Anthropic's commercial terms this data is not used to train their models. If you never use AI scouting, nothing is sent.

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or the service.

5. How long we keep information

  • Account, journal and preference data: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Push delivery records: 14 days.
  • Guest accounts that stop being used stop receiving alerts after a week, and may be removed after a long period of inactivity.
  • Backups held by our hosting provider expire on their normal schedule, usually within a few weeks.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, journals, follows, tokens and preferences.
  • Edit or delete a journal at any time in the app.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • Access, correction or a copy of your data: email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, for example in California or the European Economic Area, you may have further rights. These include asking us to access, correct, delete or move your data, objecting to some processing, and complaining to your local data protection authority. We honour these requests wherever you live. Where the GDPR applies, we process your data to provide the service you asked for (contract), for our legitimate interest in running and securing the app, and with your consent for optional features such as notifications and AI scouting.

7. Public results about you

Competition results are published by tournament organizers. If you are a fencer, or the parent of one, and want your public results corrected or hidden in Riposte, email us from the address linked to your account, or include enough detail for us to identify the record. We'll reply within 30 days.

8. Children

Fencing has youth events, so public results in Riposte can include fencers under 13. Riposte accounts are not intended for children under 13 without a parent or guardian setting them up. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information without parental consent, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored with our hosting provider. Database access rules limit each person to their own private data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you as required by law if a breach affects you.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes we'll also tell you in the app before they take effect.
