Notice to Parents

Version 2026-10-28

You are setting up Riposte for your child. Before you do, we need your consent to collect and use your child's information. This notice explains what that means. Please read it before you agree in the app.

The short version
  • We collect only what Riposte needs to work for your child.
  • We never sell it or use it for ads. The parent assistant never sees anything that identifies a child under 13.
  • Coaches can only message your child after you or your child accept their link.
  • You can see, delete or stop it at any time, in the app or by email.

1. Who we are

Riposte is an iPhone app for fencers, parents and coaches. It is run by Liam Li. You can reach us at l1y7iam@gmail.com.

2. We are asking for your consent

Children under 13 can't make a Riposte account. To use Riposte beyond looking around as a guest, a parent sets it up. When you tap to agree in the app, you consent to us collecting and using your child's information as this notice describes. If you don't agree, we won't collect it, and your child can still look around as a guest.

We record that you agreed: your account, your child's fencer profile, the date and time, and this notice's version.

3. What we collect about your child

  • To claim their profile: their fencing federation member number, last name and birth year. You enter these. We use them only to match and check the profile.
  • What their use of Riposte creates: bout journals, coach links, lesson requests and bookings, and messages with their coach.
  • For alerts: the push token Apple gives the app, if notifications are on.

Riposte also shows public competition results about your child: name, club, birth year, ratings and results. These come from askFRED, USA Fencing rankings, FencingTracker, Fencing Time Live and results files uploaded by organizers. They are shown whether or not you use Riposte.

Riposte doesn't access your child's location, contacts or photos, and has no ads, analytics or tracking.

4. How we use it

  • To show your child's events, results and alerts.
  • To keep their bout journal and sync it.
  • To connect them with their coach: links, lessons and messages.
  • To keep Riposte safe, including reviewing reports.

We don't use it for advertising. Nothing that identifies a child under 13 is ever sent to an AI service: when you ask the parent assistant about them, it sees only "Child 1" (no name, birth year or member number), their age group, weapon, region and the public competitions picked for them. Other users never see scouting tags about them.

5. No more than we need

We ask only for what a feature needs. Your child doesn't have to give more information to take part in any activity.

6. Who else gets it

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and server functions. Your child's information is stored there.
  • Apple delivers push notifications and receives the push token and the alert text.
  • Coaches your child is linked to see their lessons, their messages with them, and any journal entry your child chooses to share.

We don't share your child's information with anyone else, except if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety. We don't sell it.

7. Coaches and messages

  • A coach can only message your child after you or your child accept the coach's link, or if the coach coaches at your child's club.
  • Every chat has block and report. We review every report.
  • Coaches agree not to contact children outside Riposte's coach links.

8. Your rights

  • See it: your child's journals, coach links, bookings and messages are in the app. Email us for a full copy.
  • Withdraw consent: Profile → Settings, withdraw consent. This deletes your child's journals, coach links, lesson requests and bookings, and coach message threads from your account, and releases the claim on their profile. We stop collecting their information.
  • Delete everything: Profile → Settings → Delete account, or email us.
  • Correct or hide their public profile: email us.

Email l1y7iam@gmail.com from the address on your account. We reply within 30 days. We may ask you to show you are the parent first.

9. How long we keep it

  • Journals, coach links, lessons and messages: until you delete them, withdraw consent or delete the account.
  • Notifications in the app: 30 days after they're read or delivered, 60 days at most.
  • Push delivery records: 14 days.
  • Public competition results stay, because they are public records. You can ask us to hide your child's profile.

10. More

Our Privacy Policy has the full details, and our Terms explain the age rules. If we change this notice in a way that affects your child's information, we'll ask you to agree again.
